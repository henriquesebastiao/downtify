Downtify Spotify companion

Lets your Downtify server follow your own version of Spotify's personal mixes. Sign in to Spotify in this browser (the companion keeps a Spotify tab open for that). Your Spotify sign-in never leaves this browser: Downtify only gets song lists, plus your account's name so it can tell if someone else signs in. Downtify decides what is read and when.
